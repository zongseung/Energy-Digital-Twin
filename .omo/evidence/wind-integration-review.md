# Jeju wind integration review

Scope: read-only review of the all-Jeju-station delta in `src/weather.rs`, `src/main.rs`, and `renderers/twin/{app.js,grid.js,index.html,style.css}`, plus the real 43-row fixture and recorded browser QA. No blocking findings in the reviewed source.

- **PASS — one source poll and 43 stations.** The fixed keyless KMA Jeju batch URL is polled by one shared 60-second task; the fetch has an 8-second deadline and 1 MiB body cap. `/api/v1/jeju/wind/stations` returns the station cache; `/api/v1/jeju/wind` remains a Gosan compatibility view of that same cache. Both routes use `no-store`. The real `src/weather_fixture.json` has `stnId=0`, Jeju `sidoCode`, `isAllStn=true`, and 43 items, and the parser test accepts all 43.
- **PASS — row integrity and lifecycle.** The parser validates batch identity/unit, per-row ID/name/finite Jeju coordinates, item KST timestamp, trouble flag, 10-minute nonnegative finite speed, and the 16-point from-direction. Calm has no heading. Bad wind retains valid station metadata with `unavailable` status; a missing or invalid row retains an older reading as `stale`. A failed batch marks all retained observations stale. Freshness requires a successful current row, observation age <=15 minutes, and receipt age <=150 seconds.
- **PASS — facility selection and scene.** The browser computes great-circle distance from selected facility coordinates, selects the nearest fresh station, and shows a fallback notice if the nearest station is invalid. A fresh calm nearest station stays selected. The 43-row table includes distant Jeju islands and shows observation time and status. Each of the 10 nacelles receives its own station bearing through an ID-keyed map; tower/foundation transforms stay fixed, and stale/calm/failure/layer-off/context-loss restores original nacelle quaternions. Sea imagery remains visible and simplified buildings remain hidden by default. Source, KST time, 10-minute average, 16-point precision, demo yaw, and lack of turbine SCADA/RPM are labelled in the UI.
- **PASS — recorded browser QA.** `var/verification/buildings-wind/wind-browser-qa.json` reports 43 station rows including Chuja and Marado, independent 2 Gosan / 8 Nakcheon turbine matching, Hallim for a selected PV facility, fallback/calm behavior, four cardinal headings across all nacelles, unchanged towers/foundations, restoration states, mobile 375px layout, and WebGL context loss. Its `wind-browser-qa.mjs` is the runnable evidence.

Live all-station HTTP/UI proof: deployed `GET /api/v1/jeju/wind/stations` returned HTTP 200, `Cache-Control: no-store`, 43/43 fresh rows, KMA batch received `2026-09-29T18:17:06Z` (03:17:06 KST), with Gosan 3.7 m/s NE, Nakcheon 1.0 m/s E, and rows for Chuja and Marado. The compatibility `/wind` route returned the same Gosan reading and timestamp. `jeju-wind-mapping.json` independently computes 2 Gosan / 8 Nakcheon nearest stations from actual facility coordinates; `wind-cache-live.json` records 48 concurrent reads of one cached batch. `wind-live-browser.json` reports PASS at 1280px and 375px without fixtures: 43-row live table, Gosan 3.7 m/s NE for turbine 01, Nakcheon 1.0 m/s E for turbine 03, 10 active direction demos, unchecked buildings, 21 facilities, and no horizontal overflow. I visually inspected `wind-live-1280-all-stations.png` and `wind-live-375-nakcheon.png`: sea imagery, per-facility station and KST observation text, source link, and demo disclaimer are visible. Prior Gosan-only live checks are superseded by this expanded implementation.

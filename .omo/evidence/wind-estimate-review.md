# Wind estimate independent review (2026-09-30)

**Status: PASS, no remaining blocker.**

Reviewed `renderers/twin/wind-estimate.mjs`, `app.js`, `grid.js`, `index.html`, `renderers/mock/nginx.conf`, `tests/wind-estimate.mjs`, the research note, and `docs/estimated-twin.md`.

- The estimator implements the documented illustrative 3/13/25 m/s cut-in/rated/cut-out bands, cubic ramp `3000(v³−27)/2170` kW, and visual `min(15.7, 15.7v/13)` RPM. It rejects invalid wind speeds. The 3 MW cap and older WinDS3000/91 thresholds are described as assumptions; the displayed result is explicitly not observed generation or the manufacturer power curve. At cut-out, `0 RPM` is explicitly a visualization convention, not evidence of a stopped real rotor.
- `showWind()` uses each turbine's GIS coordinates and the existing closest fresh station selection, including the calm-nearest case. Stale, failed, hidden-tab, and connection-loss paths clear usable RPM; wind layer and WebGL context-loss paths stop animation. `syncDemo()` schedules at most one frame, and the frame callback clears its handle before rescheduling. Nacelle orientation remains based on wind-from direction; no invented yaw loss is applied. The measured Jeju footer stays on the separate state WebSocket and is unchanged by this estimate.
